What it is

Winter Wonderland is the World Equestrian Center's yearly Christmas celebration. Over four million lights cover the Grand Plaza, with Santa, the Grinch, live shows, holiday treats and shopping. Grounds open at 3 pm with a ticket, and the lights come on at 5 pm. It works as an evening day trip or as a weekend stay at the on-site hotel.

What there is to do

Every open night has the lights, Santa and the Grinch. Friday and Saturday nights add the full entertainment lineup.

Holiday lights

Every night

A life-sized lighted horse and carriage, a walk-through Christmas tree and photo spots all over the Grand Plaza.

Meet Santa

Every night

Keepsake photo and a letter-writing station in Indoor Arena 2.

The Grinch's lair

Every night

Photos with the Grinch, Cindy Lou Who, Thing 1 and Thing 2 in Indoor Arena 1.

Virtual reality rides

Every night

New this year: a train to the North Pole and a magical sleigh ride. $8 add-on per person.

Stage shows & performers

Fri, Sat & Dec 20–23

Elves on the main stage, an LED glow show, a drum show, stilt walkers, princesses, face painting and caricature artists.

Line dancing lessons

Fri, Sat & Dec 20–23

Under the lights outside Miss Tilly's Lollipops.

Hot cocoa & holiday bites

Fri, Sat & Dec 20–23

Hot cocoa and seasonal treats on the plaza, plus cash bars for the grown-ups.

Holiday sweets

Daily through Dec 31

Holiday pies and Yule logs at Emma's Patisserie, cookie-decorating kits at Miss Tilly's.

Weekend by weekend

Every weekend has the same core activities. What changes is the crowd, the price and the horse shows.

Dec 2–6
Full entertainment Fri 4 and Sat 5. December show jumping begins on the grounds.
Dec 9–13
Full entertainment Fri 11 and Sat 12. The higher-level international show jumping week.
Dec 16–20
Full entertainment Fri 18 and Sat 19. Sun 20 has weekend-level entertainment and pricing.
Dec 21–23Mon–Wed
Final nights before Christmas, open every night with full entertainment and weekend pricing. The busiest stretch.

Wednesdays, Thursdays and Sundays are cheaper and quieter but have no stage shows or cocoa stands. Exact show jumping days are on the show schedule.

Two ways to visit

Evening day trip

Drive up in the afternoon, see the lights from 5 to 9 pm, and drive home, about 90 minutes each way.

The least expensive option, but it makes for a late night.

Stay at the hotel

About $400 / night

Two-night minimum, so roughly $800 plus taxes for a weekend at The Equestrian Hotel, right next to the lights and known for its elaborate Christmas lobby.

The Stay Enchanted package adds:

  • Winter Wonderland tickets and VR rides
  • $100 dining credit per room
  • A commemorative Christmas ornament
  • 15% off at the A Sudden Impulse boutique
  • An in-room teepee camp for kids
